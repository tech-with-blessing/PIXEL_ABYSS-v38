### 1. Level Structure
Levels utilize a 64x64 grid system with diverse tile types (stone, grass, mud, brick). The architecture supports multi-layered platforms, providing interesting movement opportunities for up to 4 players. Moving platforms and water zones (normal, toxic, healing, lava) add dynamic elements to the traversal.

### 2. Spawn Point Fairness
Player spawns are handled via `"type": "start"` entries. While basic levels use a single `(x, y)` coordinate, more advanced levels (e.g., Level 10) define a `"points"` array (e.g., `[[0, 64], [0, -64]]`) to facilitate balanced spawns for multiple players. Ensuring all levels utilize distinct points is critical for fairness in 4-player sessions.

### 3. Difficulty Flow
Difficulty is scaled through `entity settings` embedded in each level's JSON, adjusting enemy health and attack power. The progression from Level 1 to 12 involves increasing environmental hazards and enemy density, though specific "spikes" were observed in levels with high concentrations of TNT and Archer enemies.

### 4. Chokepoints
Narrow corridors are frequently formed by "stone_inside" block clusters. These act as tactical bottlenecks. Placement of Archer or TNT enemies near these exits currently creates high-pressure zones that may require tuning to avoid unfair player domination.

### 5. Verticality
The game makes good use of vertical space, with tilesets specifically designed for tops and sides of platforms. The inclusion of swimming mechanics and "jump" logic in `game/main.py` suggests that verticality is a core pillar of the level design.

### 6. Replayability
Replayability is high due to the class-based system (5 distinct player types) and the integrated World Editor, which allows for rapid iteration and custom level creation.

### Concrete Improvements
- **Mandatory Multi-Spawn:** Standardize the `"points"` array across all levels to prevent player overlapping.
- **Hazard Variety:** Introduce more "healing" water zones as rewards for reaching difficult vertical peaks.
- **Chokepoint Relief:** Add destructible "deco" objects in narrow corridors to provide alternative paths or cover.
