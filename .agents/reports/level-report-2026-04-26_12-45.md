# Level Design Report: The Glitched Core

## Overview
"The Glitched Core" is a medium-sized level designed for 4 players, focusing on verticality and hazard management. It features a central toxic zone and an upper lava pit, forcing players to navigate carefully while dealing with enhanced enemies.

## Design Goals
- **Multiplayer Balance:** Four distinct spawn points (`type: "start"`) are distributed across two tiers to prevent immediate player crowding.
- **Hazard Integration:** 
    - A **Toxic Water** pool at the bottom (`y: 192`) serves as a penalty for falling.
    - A **Lava** pit at the top (`y: -448`) guards the central high-ground.
- **Structural Variety:** 
    - **Stone** blocks form the lower side platforms.
    - **Brick** blocks form the mid-tier platforms.
    - **Grass** blocks form the central bridge over the toxic pool.
- **Enhanced Difficulty:** Entity settings for Pawns, Warriors, and Archers have been buffed (higher health and attack power) to provide a "Glitched Core" challenge.

## Entity Configuration
- **Pawns:** Increased health (100) and attack (6).
- **Warriors:** Heavy hitters with 120 health and 8 attack power.
- **Archers:** Long-range threats with 110 health and 10 attack power.

## Layout Summary
- **Lower Tier:** Stone platforms at x: -640 and x: 640.
- **Mid Tier:** Brick platforms at x: -320 and x: 320.
- **Center:** A central column of stone blocks with a grass bridge below it.
- **Hazards:** Toxic water below the bridge, Lava above the mid-tier brick platforms.
