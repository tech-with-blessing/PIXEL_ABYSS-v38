### 1. Level Structure
The levels (e.g., `levels/1.json`, `levels/2.json`) are composed of a high density of "block" types, creating complex platforming environments. While providing numerous movement opportunities, the density in files like `1.json` suggests highly detailed but potentially cluttered environments that may impede fast-paced 4-player movement.

### 2. Spawn Point Fairness
Analysis of `game/main.py` and `levels/*.json` shows that most levels define a single `"type": "start"` point. This causes all players to spawn at the same coordinates, leading to immediate overlap and potential "spawn-fragging" in a 4-player setting. This is a significant balance issue.

### 3. Difficulty Flow
Levels progress from relatively simple block layouts to environments with extensive "water" zones (found in `levels/6.json`, `7.json`, and `8.json`). The introduction of "toxic" and "lava" zones in later levels provides a natural increase in environmental difficulty.

### 4. Chokepoints
The use of large "stone" and "grass" blocks often creates narrow corridors. In Level 1, the heavy use of "tree" and "deco" items alongside structural blocks creates potential chokepoints where players may get stuck or where "Archer" and "TNT" enemies can dominate from a distance.

### 5. Verticality
Verticality is present through layered platform structures. The code in `game/main.py` handles camera and viewport logic that supports vertical exploration, and the level bounds (varying Y coordinates) indicate that vertical space is used, though player movement is heavily reliant on jumping between relatively small platforms.

### 6. Replayability
Replayability is encouraged by the variety of enemy types (Pawn, Archer, TNT, Torch, Warrior) and their randomized attributes defined in the "entity settings" section of each level. Environmental hazards like rising water levels (seen in `game/main.py` water zone logic) add dynamic elements.

### Concrete Improvements & Ideas
- **Multi-Player Spawns:** Update the level schema to support an array of `start_points` instead of a single point. Modify `game/main.py` to distribute players 1-4 across these points.
- **Platform Variety:** Introduce moving platforms more consistently in early levels to bridge gaps, reducing reliance on high-precision jumping.
- **Zone Balancing:** Ensure "healing" water zones are placed in contested areas to encourage movement and tactical positioning.
- **Level Idea:** A "Vertical Abyss" level focusing on a deep shaft with crumbling platforms (using short-lived blocks) to force constant upward or downward movement.
